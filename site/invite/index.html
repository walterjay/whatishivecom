Apply for a free invite

Takes about two minutes. In a hurry? You can sign up instantly with a free app instead.

How it works

  1. Apply

    Fill in a short form: your email, the username you’d like, and what you’d post about first. A link to a profile or site of yours (Instagram, YouTube, a blog) is optional, but those requests are reviewed first.

  2. I review every request by hand

    I read each one myself and approve people in small weekly batches, so I can actually help everyone who joins. The pilot is limited to 100 people. If you’re approved, I’ll email you.

  3. You create your own keys

    My email will send you to Ecency’s “Invited by a friend” sign-up. You pick your username there, your keys are created on your own device, and you save them. You then send me a link, and I create the account from it.

    I never see your private keys and I will never ask for them. Anyone who does is not me.

  4. Your first week

    Write a short post introducing yourself, tagged introduceyourself. Once you’ve posted, I lend your account extra capacity so you can post and comment freely, and I’ll be there to answer questions.

Good to know

  • It’s free, with no strings. The account is created with account-creation tokens that my own Hive account earns. You don’t pay anything and don’t owe anything.
  • The account is fully yours. Your keys control it, not me. It works in every Hive app, not only the one you signed up with.
  • I’ll be your recovery account. Because I create the account, Hive makes me its recovery account by default: if someone steals your account, I can help you get it back within 30 days. You can switch to a different recovery account later.
  • Your details stay private. The form is hosted by Tally. I use your email only to contact you about your invite, never share it, and will delete your request if you ask.
  • Want to stay anonymous? Skip the form and use a paid option that needs no email, such as Hivedex, from signup.hive.io.
